🏠 Manatee PersonalHome – Complete Analysis

FL Select Homeowners (LOB = PersonalHome) | New Business Quote → Bind
ProductBase: Manatee_ProductBase_PersonalLines_1_0_0_0 + _2_0_0_0
LOB Manuscript: Manatee_PersonalHome_Peril_1_0_0_0

State: Florida
Analysis Date: September 2026

🎯 What is Manatee PersonalHome?

  • FL Select Homeowners product on the Duck Creek platform
  • Covers HO3 (Homeowner) and HO6 (Condominium) policy forms
  • Managed by Manatee carrier within the Duck Creek ecosystem
  • Rides on a shared ProductBase PersonalLines framework
  • LOB-specific behaviour defined in Manatee_PersonalHome_Peril_1_0_0_0

📑 Scope of This Analysis

  • Complete New Business Quote → Bind page flow (11 main steps + Entry & Bind)
  • All navigable ViewModels and popup collections
  • Field-level details for 9 analysed pages
  • ProductBase vs LOB inheritance mapping
  • Field dependencies, conditions, and validation rules
  • Model Collections: MainInterview, ClueClaimHistoryDetail, SubscriberAgreement

🗺️ Overall Page Structure

  • ENTRY – New Quote Selection (DCT System)
  • Pages 1-2 – Applicant & Policy Information (ProductBase)
  • Pages 3-4 – Risk Schedule & Dwelling Coverage (LOB)
  • Pages 5-6 – Underwriting & Insurance History (ProductBase)
  • Pages 7-7B – Claims History & CLUE Report (ProductBase)
  • Pages 8-11 – Coverage Summary, Interests, Billing (PB + DCT Billing)
  • BIND – Commit Transaction

🔗 Key Integrations

  • Maprisk / PBBI: Geocoding, territory, lat/long, fire protection
  • LexisNexis: CLUE report + NCF insurance score
  • Verisk 360: Replacement cost valuation
  • Swiss Re Flood: Flood scoring
  • Zesty AI: Aerial photo capture
  • IVANS: Download to agency management system
  • Insvista: Notifications (applicable LOBs)
  • Data Insights: Quote/bind business intelligence

⚡ Important Flow Conditions

  • Page 7B (CLUE Report) only appears when CLUE ran & returned ≥1 claim
  • Underwriting page variant depends on LOB: FL Select, FL Advantage, LA Select, US Advantage
  • Make A Payment & Billing Summary only shown when DCT Billing is enabled
  • Rating runs automatically when Coverage Summary loads (calculate="2")
  • Subscriber Agreement popup required before Bind on new business
  • Transfer To Agency page only visible when ImportAgencyDNE = True & user is SPAgencyAdmin

📐 Architecture Summary

  • 3 Model Collections: MainInterview (primary), ClueClaimHistoryDetail (popup), SubscriberAgreement (popup)
  • 26 ViewModels in MainInterview (pages + popups)
  • PersonalHome LOB adds: RiskSchedule + DwellingCoverage ViewModels
  • ProductBase defines all other pages; LOB can suppress or override
  • PolicyDetail page is suppressed in PersonalHome LOB (always hidden)
  • PricingPage is suppressed in PersonalHome LOB (Coverage Summary used instead)

🗂️ Model Collections & ViewModels

The Manatee PersonalHome application uses 3 Model Collections. MainInterview is the parent primary collection that drives the entire workflow. The other two are popup/modal collections launched on-demand. The PersonalHome LOB manuscript does not define its own separate model collections — it extends the MainInterview collection defined in ProductBase.
